# Geoff and Cindi

A family advent calendar (a door a day through November and December) and a two-player sudoku, made for Geoff and Cindi. No ads, no accounts, nothing to install.

- `index.html`: the calendar. Doors open on their own day; past doors can be reopened; future doors stay locked.
- `sudoku.html`: the sudoku, opened from a door. Race together or play when you can and compare times. Moves and times pass through free public MQTT relays, encrypted with the family's own code, which is only in the invite link.

Built from the family's private source; changes are pushed here by Claude when Guy asks.
