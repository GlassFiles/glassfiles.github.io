Connecting a keyboard

1. Find the port on the back of the keyboard.

  • A square USB port (often marked "USB to Host" or "Computer"): the easy one. Use a USB-B to USB-C cable for most Android devices and newer computers, or USB-B to USB-A for older computers.
  • Round 5-pin sockets marked MIDI OUT: use a USB-MIDI lead. Its IN plug goes into the keyboard's MIDI OUT.
  • Only headphone or audio sockets: no cable route. Use the microphone instead.

2. Turn the keyboard on and plug it into this device. Open this page in Chrome (on Android or a computer), tap Connect keyboard, and allow it when asked.

3. Press a key. The card shows the note it heard. If nothing shows, unplug it, plug it back in and tap Connect again.

4. The keyboard makes its own sound, so the app's note sound turns off when connected.

iPhone and iPad: Safari can't read a keyboard by cable, so use the microphone. Bluetooth keyboards weren't tried in this test version, so use a cable.
